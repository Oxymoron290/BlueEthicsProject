Pursuant to the Texas Public Information Act, Texas Government Code Chapter 552, I respectfully request copies of the following public records:

I request records sufficient to show the total and itemized cost of each election conducted by, for, or on behalf of the City of Bedford from January 1, 2019 through the present, including but not limited to:

- General municipal elections (mayor and city council)
- Special elections
- Runoff elections
- Charter amendment elections
- Bond elections

For each election during this period, please provide:

1. The total cost incurred by the City.
2. An itemized breakdown of expenditures, including but not limited to:
  a. Payments to Tarrant County or other election administrators
  b. Poll worker compensation
  c. Early voting costs
  d. Ballot printing
  e. Mail ballot processing and postage
  f. Equipment programming, rental, or technical services
  g. Legal notice publication
  h. Translation services
  i. Security or facility rental
  j. Administrative or overhead charges
3. Any reimbursements, shared-cost allocations, or offsets received.
4. Copies of any interlocal agreements, contracts, or cost-sharing agreements in effect during this time period governing election administration.
5. The total number of ballots cast and the number of registered voters for each election.
6. Any internal summaries, reports, memoranda, invoices, or council agenda materials discussing election costs for the above elections.

If responsive information exists in electronic format, I request it in electronic form.

If the estimated cost of fulfilling this request exceeds $40.00, please provide a written, itemized estimate before proceeding.

If any portion of this request is denied, please cite the specific statutory exception under Chapter 552 that authorizes withholding and identify the name and title of the person responsible for the determination.

Please notify me if you anticipate that production will exceed the statutory response timeframe so that we may coordinate delivery.
